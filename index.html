This Is Not Just Another Sailing Holiday

Wild Mediterranean islands and protected marine parks sit within sailing distance of vineyards, contemporary art, Saint-Tropez beach life, and the glamour of Cannes. We combine real sailing, island exploration, friendly competition, crew challenges, and vibrant social events into one floating community.

5 Unforgettable Experiences

  • Sailing the Riviera: Experience the iconic coastline directly from the water.
  • The Flotilla Challenge: Competitive catamaran race from Cap Taillat to Cap Camarat.
  • The Wild Islands: Biking & wine in Porquerolles; snorkelling in Port-Cros Marine Park.
  • The Floating Club: Signature rafted Flotilla Party with live DJ in a sheltered bay.
  • Cannes & Estérel: Sunset views over Cannes and the striking red cliffs of Estérel.
PLANNED ROUTE: Port Pin Rolland → Giens Peninsula → Porquerolles → Port-Cros → Île du Levant → Cap Taillat → Pampelonne → Cannes / Îles de Lérins → Estérel Coast → Port Pin Rolland

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Welcome to the South of France Saturday, 26 June
Arrival at Port Pin Rolland on the Saint-Mandrier peninsula near Toulon. Crews settle into their modern Bali catamarans, complete handovers, and stock supplies.
Afternoon
Arrival, crew introductions, cabin allocation, and supermarket provisioning.
Evening
Welcome Reception at Le Carré Plage (Les Sablettes). Barefoot drinks & identity selection.
Night
Overnight at berth in Port Pin Rolland.
Day 2: Cast Off & Giens Peninsula Sunday, 27 June
Early departure towards the Giens Peninsula. A dedicated sailing day to get familiar with the Bali catamarans and fall into the rhythm of the sea.
Morning
Cast off towards Giens. First swim stop at Calanque du Blé.
Afternoon
Coastal sailing around the peninsula with sheltered anchorages for lunch.
Evening
Sunset anchorage and crew dinner under the stars.
Day 3: Bicycles, Art & Island Rosé Monday, 28 June
Sails down and bicycles out in Porquerolles. Explore pine forests, secluded beaches, contemporary art at Villa Carmignac, and local Provençal wine tastings.
Morning
Bike collection, island exploration, and visit to Villa Carmignac art estate.
Afternoon
Provençal wine tasting at local vineyards before returning to the boats.
Evening
Overnight anchorage at Plage Notre-Dame / Baie d’Alicastre.
Day 4: National Parks & Golden Hour Tuesday, 29 June
Experience the wild side of the Riviera in Port-Cros National Park. Features the Bali Skipper Exchange and underwater trail snorkelling at La Palud.
Morning
Optional beach yoga followed by the Bali Skipper Exchange leg.
Afternoon
Snorkelling the marine trail at La Palud / La Gabinière reserve.
Evening
Quiet wilderness anchorage at Port-Man / Calanque du Palangrier.
Day 5: Cap Taillat & The Flotilla Party Wednesday, 30 June
Sail past Île du Levant and the frigate Jean Bart to Cap Taillat for a sunset walk, professional photoshoot, and the signature floating party.
Morning
Coastal passage past Île du Levant & naval breakwater viewings.
Afternoon
Hike to Cap Taillat summit & professional flotilla photography session.
Night
The Signature Flotilla Party: Rafted fleet / circle anchorage with DJ.
Day 6: Race Day, Pampelonne & Cannes Thursday, 1 July
High energy! The New Generation Flotilla Cup race towards Cap Camarat, followed by the hilarious Pampelonne Dinghy Challenge and evening arrival in Cannes.
Morning
Flotilla Catamaran Race: Cap Taillat to Cap Camarat.
Afternoon
Pampelonne Dinghy Challenge (Costumes & Rowing) at Saint-Tropez.
Evening
Passage to Îles de Lérins / Cannes. Potential pyrotechnic display views.
Day 7: The Estérel & Farewell Seafood Dinner Friday, 2 July
Sail past the striking red cliffs of the Estérel Coast back towards Port Pin Rolland. Final refueling stop at Porquerolles followed by the official awards gala.
Morning
Scenic passage along the red rock coast of Estérel & swim stop.
Afternoon
Refueling stop at Porquerolles and final leg to Port Pin Rolland base.
Evening
Seafood dinner & Flotilla Cup Awards at L’Écrin dégustation (Tamaris Bay).
Day 8: Departure / Au Revoir Saturday, 3 July
Breakfast aboard, final packing, and check-out by 09:00. Optional private transfers to Nice Côte d’Azur Airport or onward travel in Provence.
Morning
Check-out by 09:00 & group luggage transfer assistance.
Afternoon
Onward travel to Nice Airport, Provence, or Monaco.
Departure
Au revoir, South of France!

Optional Crewed Upgrades & APA

Stewardess Hire (€1,600 / week)

Includes daily boat cleaning, breakfast preparation, and light afternoon snacks served on board.

Private Chef Hire (€2,500 / week)

Dedicated onboard chef catering 3 full meals per day crafted around local Provençal seafood and specialties.

APA (Advance Provisioning Allowance) Note

Boats opting for additional hired crew (Stewardess and/or Chef) are subject to a 30% APA Surcharge calculated on the total boat charter value. The APA covers onboard food provisioning, beverages, fuel, port fees, and specific crew logistics.
